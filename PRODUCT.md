# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active job seekers and passive career explorers. Active users are managing 10–50+ applications across companies, juggling interview rounds, follow-up dates, and resume versions — they need pipeline visibility and follow-up discipline. Passive users browse opportunities and want a clean way to save and compare roles without ceremony.

Both groups share one context: the job search is uncertain and emotionally taxing. Orbit is a steadying presence, not another source of urgency.

## Product Purpose

Orbit helps job seekers stop losing track of applications. It keeps every job, every contact, and every deadline organized in one place. Success looks like a user who never misses a follow-up, can see their pipeline at a glance, and lands their next role faster because they're not wasting energy on admin overhead.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, sharp, calm.** Professional without being stuffy, clean without being cold. The navy foundation signals stability and competence; the teal accent adds a human, optimistic note. Copy is direct and reassuring — no gamification, no hype, no "you're killing it" as a UX pattern.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS cream — the standard light-gray dashboard palette (HubSpot, Salesforce, Asana). Muted and forgettable. Orbit should never feel like just another B2B tool. Each surface should have presence: the deep primary takes space, the charts feel editorial, the cards are structured but not uniform.

## Design Principles

**Confidence without noise.** The navy/teal palette is assured but not flashy. Every element earns its place; nothing is decorative for the sake of filling space. Cards have presence through structure and typography, not through borders and shadows layered together.

**Sharp clarity.** Clear hierarchy, crisp type (Manrope headlines, Inter body), generous whitespace. The Kanban view and table view coexist because they serve different mental models, not because more views is better. Labels and statuses are immediately scannable.

**Calm control.** Job search is anxious. Orbit counters that with a steady, predictable interface. Motion is intentional and exponential-eased, never bouncy. Navigation is consistent. The unsaved-changes detector and auto-save patterns reassure without demanding attention.

**Pipeline as narrative.** The Sankey chart, funnel, and activity feed tell the story of progress, not just data. Every visualization answers a question the user actually has: where am I stuck? what's working? what needs attention?

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 against its background, large text ≥3:1. Reduced motion respected throughout — every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Keyboard-navigable via Radix UI primitives. Color not the only differentiator for status badges.
